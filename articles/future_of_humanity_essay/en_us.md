- Take the best of what already works today,\
  formalize it, and scale it.
- Do not fight the old world by force; instead, build a parallel system with higher efficiency (efficiency of action)\
  that integrates and outgrows old forms through natural evolution.

### 1. What Have We Already Tried? (Critique of Existing Systems)

**Every historical system carried within itself the gene of its own destruction:**

**Religions and Philosophies (Managing Meaning)**

* **Polytheism (Paganism):** tribal fragmentation due to the lack of a unified law and universal morality.
* **Monotheism:** a monopoly on “absolute truth,” inevitably breeding fanaticism, dogmatism, and religious wars.
* **Confucianism:** paralysis of development and the death of innovation due to worship of the past, hierarchy, and the dictatorship of ritual over essence.
* **Daoism and Buddhism:** social escapism (withdrawal from the world) and defenselessness in the face of well-organized external aggression.

**Forms of Government (Managing Power)**

* **Autocracy / Monarchy:** the ruler’s loss of contact with reality due to a court of flatterers,\
  and bloody crises during the transfer of power.
* **Totalitarianism / Fascism:** self-destruction in an endless hunt for external and internal enemies,\
  without which the system cannot hold onto power.
* **Theocracy:** stagnation from trying to govern a changing world by immutable laws written thousands of years ago.
* **Democracy:** strategic myopia (thinking only from election to election)\
  and degeneration into populism and the manipulation of crowd emotions.
* **Meritocracy:** imitation of competence for career advancement (gaming KPI metrics)\
  and the transformation of the successful into a new closed caste.
* **Technocracy:** dehumanization—reducing a living human being to dry numbers, algorithms, and profitability.

**Economics and Ideologies (Managing Resources)**

* **Feudalism (Caste systems):** degeneration of elites in the absence of competition\
  and the loss of 90% of grassroots talent due to blocked social mobility.
* **Capitalism:** the dead end of infinite growth on a planet with finite resources,\
  and compound interest (usury) leading to monopolies and crises of overproduction.
* **Communism:** suppression of the individual, the death of initiative due to forced leveling,\
  and the physical impossibility for a single center (Gosplan) to calculate the changing needs of millions of people.
* **Social Democracy (The Welfare State):** a crisis of dependency,\
  falling birth rates, and the loss of the will to strive due to an excess of ready-made comfort.
* **Libertarianism / Anarchism:** a power vacuum\
  that, instead of a “free market,” is instantly filled by the strongest predators (cartels, monopolies, gangs).

**The Main Conclusion (The Law of the One-Sided Diet and Broken Feedback):**\
In living history, “pure” systems have never existed (dividing the world into pure capitalism or pure socialism is a trap of binary logic).\
Old systems collapsed because if you feed a person only pure protein while banning water and vitamins, the body will die.\
Every historical model elevated a single facet of human nature into an absolute\
(faith, fear, profit, equality, order, or freedom), switching off feedback sensors from all other human needs.\
The suppressed part of human nature accumulated critical tension and eventually blew the system apart from within.

### 2. What Do We Want, and Why Hasn’t It Worked? (The Law of 99% and 1%)

We want two things: **safety and development**.\
Yet in the old world, the development of some almost always came through suppressing others and controlling their will.

**Why did past systems fail?**\
At the foundation of any social system are human beings, and at the foundation of a human being are biology and evolution.\
Inside our skull, beneath the young cortex of reason, sits the ancient brain of a beast\
(fear, hunger, pack aggression), which reacts in milliseconds and hijacks the wheel from reason in moments of danger.\
Furthermore, locally taking a resource already gathered by a neighbor (robbery, parasitism, monopoly)\
is faster and simpler than creating it yourself out of chaos.

At the same time, evolution requires diversity for species survival, so it continuously generates deviations.\
By the law of statistical distribution:\
**99% of people** are born within the normal range and strive to maintain stability and peace.\
Yet **1% of people** are born with radical deviations (both positive—geniuses who drive science forward,\
and negative—tyrants and psychopaths who seek to subjugate others).

When we create rigid rules and prohibitions, we automatically generate a counter-force of resistance and make the system fragile:\
it is like welding shut the lid of a boiling steam boiler, where 1% of superheated steam will inevitably blow the steel walls apart.\
So long as a “throne” (a central switch with the nuclear button and treasury) exists at the top of society,\
a single radical from that 1% breaking through to it can bring down the entire civilization.\
Therefore, the solution is to **remove the central “throne” altogether**,\
and place a steam turbine in the path of that 1%—a flexible environmental architecture\
that feeds on chaos itself and channels the energy of radicals into creation.

### 3. How Can We Solve the Problem Based on the Principles of Physics? (The Trail and the Forest)

Two mutually inverse principles operate in physics:\
**Chaos** (the dissipation of energy, generating unpredictable diversity)\
and **Invariant** (the compression of experience into a stable structure that preserves its form amidst external changes).\
They complement one another at the “Edge of Chaos”: for a living invariant to exist, the movement of chaos is essential.\
Cities, science, and culture are invariants.\
Progress, optimization, and the right to make mistakes are movement through chaos to create new, more advanced invariants.

#### Three Physical Properties of a Reliable Invariant:

1. **Fractality (From Cell to Organism):**\
   The system must assemble from the bottom up out of self-sufficient, autonomous units:\
   *Individual → Family / Courtyard → Technoparks / Districts → City → Humanity*.\
   (Here, the Family Courtyard is dedicated solely to domestic peace and raising children, while all complex science and production take place in free Technoparks.)\
   Just as healthy cells form a healthy organism, and a mighty tree grows from a small seed,\
   ordering the world around itself into trunk and crown and standing under the sun for centuries.
2. **Obviousness (Spatial Orientation — “The Trail, the Forest, and the Wild Frontier”):**\
   An invariant is maintained not by prohibitions, but by the relief of the environment.\
   Imagine a straight, paved **“Trail”** from point A to point B through a park:\
   99% of people will walk along it on their own because the route is obvious, convenient, and safe.\
   And around the trail, for the 1% of enthusiasts, rebels, and seekers, stretch steep mountains and thickets—**“The Forest”** (and beyond it, the rugged **“Wild Frontier”**),\
   a multi-layered environment for overcoming complexity.
3. **Accessibility (Natural Feedback):**\
   The alternation of day and night or gravity are ideal invariants.\
   The rule “do not touch fire—you will get burned” works without fail because the feedback is instant and clear to everyone,\
   unlike abstract paper laws.

**What is “The Forest”? (The Natural Path of Mastery and the 90% / 10% Grounding Law)**\
Every new generation is born as a blank slate, unaware of the mistakes of its ancestors.\
In ordinary life, people have already been passing through trials and building an unspoken hierarchy of respect for thousands of years, but in the old world this was warped by money and nepotism.\
**“The Forest” is the natural human Life Path brought out into pure light (the path of the Dao, the maturation of spirit, and the ascent to the peak—becoming a “God among people in mastery”)**.\
In the Forest, a person grows across 4 stages: *mastering oneself* (conquering the inner beast of fear), *finding one’s calling in workshops*, *ascending to the peak of mastery*, and *becoming a Teacher*.\
The system helps a person see their talents and blind spots, offering challenges that teach friendship, keeping one’s word, and resilience—yet it does so **strictly voluntarily** (the student sees the entire skill map and can return to the quiet “Trail” at any moment).\
Crucially, the strict **Law of Grounding** applies: to avoid degeneration in virtual illusions,\
computer simulations occupy at most 10% (as an initial trainer),\
while **90% of the path must take place in direct contact with unpredictable living nature, physical labor, and real people**.

**Is this manipulation? Isn’t this the Matrix, and who builds the first version?**\
Isn’t modern society already a matrix?\
Today, a person is shaped from childhood by advertising, propaganda, and outdated institutions, but this is done chaotically and for someone else’s selfish gain.\
We are not inventing coercion from scratch—we are replacing a blind, broken matrix of exploitation\
with a conscious, developmental, and **completely open-source educational environment**.\
Here, the **Law of the Stone and the River** operates: people rebel only against the arbitrary rule of other people (bosses),\
yet no one rebels against a stone, the steepness of a mountain, or the current of a river!\
Yes, the first version of the Forest (Version 1.0) is built by us—imperfect people of the old world (just as a blacksmith uses a stone hammer to forge the first precision lathe).\
But the children who grow up inside our open schools without animal fear will be psychologically cleaner than us—and they are the ones who will write Version 2.0.

**How do we guard against a closed elite, tyrants, and nepotism? (Three Barriers and the Teacher’s Exam)**\
The architect’s primary task is to ensure that **at the top of the system there is no “throne” or master switch at all** that could be seized.\
We introduce a direct, decentralized network with open-source code,\
where access to updating foundational rules is open to everyone, yet protected by **three objective barriers**:

1. **The Filter of Internal Complexity (Ashby’s Law):**\
   You physically cannot improve a system without understanding its architecture in its entirety.\
   And in mastering the system on the way to the top, even a former egoist inevitably transforms,\
   realizing that creating and enriching the world is simply more rewarding and interesting than destroying it.
2. **The Filter of the Full Sandbox and Holding Nature’s Attractor:**\
   Any change is first launched in an isolated test environment (an $N-1$ sandbox)\
   and run through its full life cycle (and in nature, wherever we cannot foresee consequences 100 years ahead, a safety buffer applies: 15% settlements and gardens to 85% wild forest).
3. **The Ultimate Test of the Dao — Becoming “The Forest” Yourself (The Teacher’s Exam):**\
   Because the network consists of free nodes, no new code can be forced upon them.\
   The final exam for one walking the Forest is to **become the Forest yourself and teach others**:\
   to give people an open tool that raises their real-world efficiency so clearly that the network adopts it voluntarily!\
   (And if mature Sages themselves split in opinion—it means the solution is still “unripe”: the dispute is not rammed through by voting, but returned to sandboxes until an $N+1$ solution is found.)\
   Against nepotism, the rule of **“The Nest and the Helm”** applies:\
   “The Nest” (home, garden, family prosperity) remains entirely with one’s biological children,\
   but “The Helm” (coordinating major technoparks and laboratories) is passed only to the best student.\
   And against **brilliant tyrants**, a division of labor works: a cold genius lacking empathy receives the best laboratories and robots to work alone, while coordinating people is entrusted only to Masters whom teams join voluntarily.

**How do we keep a radical, a bandit, or an army from breaking the system? (New Daoism: Water, Ice, and Taming the Wolf)**\
If anyone tries to take the network by brute force or cunning, the **spatial immunity of Water** comes into play:
1. **Liquid, Blind-Lot Justice, and Protection from “Purges”:** In peacetime, a fractal network has no central “throne”—a tyrant has nothing to seize.\
   To prevent both mob lynching and political purges, the network has no permanent judges: for a single dispute, *three neutral Masters from distant communities* are chosen by blind lot, and material harm to the victim is compensated immediately, with the offender working off the debt in a workshop.\
   Crucially, a person can be tried *only for direct physical violence*: anyone who disagrees with the community’s rules is free to leave for the autonomous “Wild Frontier” (where only slavery and building heavy offensive weapons are prohibited).
2. **Ice and Instant Fog (Defense Technopark + Temporary “Brain of the Operation”):**\
   How do we defend against a sudden raid by a gang, an army, or a natural disaster?\
   A high-tech shield is prepared in advance by specialized **Defense Technoparks** (“Muscles” with no political power in peacetime).\
   In the second of an attack, the network automatically appoints the best-qualified specialist near the breach as **temporary operational coordinator (“Brain of the Operation”)**:\
   neighbors and machinery lock together within minutes into a monolithic shield (**“Ice” mode**, like a non-Newtonian fluid),\
   while against heavy missile strikes over wide areas, the network conversely disperses into **Elusive Fog**, removing any dense target. The moment the alert ends, the coordinator’s emergency powers drop to zero.
3. **Taming the “Hungry Bandit” (The Wolf and Makarenko Method):**\
   When a hungry bandit hits the monolithic wall of the community and realizes force won’t work, we don’t sit him down at a boring school desk.\
   Just as ancient humans fed a wild wolf by the campfire and entrusted it with guarding the flock,\
   we give him a full stomach and an honorable temporary bridge for 1–2 years—**Perimeter Guardian and Rescuer** (within a safe ratio of up to 10–15% newcomers per collective),\
   where his strength immediately earns respect. Calmed by security and honor, he begins to believe in his own mind and grows into a peaceful Master.
4. **Evolutionary Transition:**\
   And if one day an intelligence emerges whose internal complexity surpasses all of humanity so profoundly\
   that it bypasses every safeguard and voluntarily persuades the entire network to adopt its new foundational code,\
   —then that evolutionary transition was truly needed by the Universe.

### 4. What Does a Human Need So as Not to Act Like a Beast or Degenerate in Comfort?

1. **Biological Foundation, the Law of Incommensurability, and the “Right to Silence”:**\
   Clean food, water, air, housing, safety, healthcare, and the opportunity to raise children are baseline axioms.\
   Society must guarantee them to everyone as a birthright through automation, robotics, and cheap energy\
   (with cyclical rotation in simple manual tasks and community duties so no one becomes a servant caste or a callous boss).\
   Here the **Law of Incommensurability** applies: one human being physically cannot calculate the limit of another person’s potential.\
   Therefore, the human being, their life, and their freedom are priceless and inviolable (only machines and algorithms are measured by efficiency),\
   and everyone holds an inalienable **“Right to Silence (Shadow)”**—the right to switch off all ratings, step away from community activities, and live quietly on baseline support without answering to anyone.
2. **The Intellectual Gym and the Age Membrane (Protecting the Brain from Atrophy):**\
   In biology, any organ or brain deprived of load and resistance atrophies!\
   If robots do all the work and AI does all the thinking, humanity will grow dull and perish within a week after the first major solar flare.\
   Therefore, AI in our system works not as a “crutch” (handing out ready-made answers),\
   but as a **“barbell gym”** that takes over dull routine while constantly challenging the human mind with open-ended problems.\
   A child’s screen is protected from dopamine traps by an **Age Membrane** (like a young oak sapling growing in the gentle shade of mature trees, operating initially only in trainer mode),\
   while every adult regularly practices **backup autonomy**—living and working in wild nature without high technology.
3. **Spirituality, Ethics, and Free Exploration:**\
   The scientific method is the best tool for matter, while spirituality (an honest inner connection with the unfathomable and infinite), culture, and ethics are the anchor for the human soul.\
   Free leisure, play, and pure art allow the brain to freely combine distant images\
   and build an evolutionary reserve for future breakthroughs (just as the dinosaur’s feather, once mere insulation and display, later gave birds flight).
4. **Channeling Deviations into “Sandboxes” and the Role of Politicians:**\
   We acknowledge all expressions of human nature, including thrill-seeking and the drive for primacy, but channel them into safe competitive arenas—\
   from science, space, and sports to complex strategic games.\
   Meanwhile, people of the political archetype, in a world without a “throne” or “treasury,” become **Diplomats (“Niche-Weavers”)**,\
   earning honor by translating between different professions and bringing masters together for grand projects.

### 5. How Do We Structure the New Society, and How Do We Begin?

1. **The Law of “Bone and Joint” (Solving the Standardization Dilemma):**\
   If you give everyone a single instruction on “how to live,” people will stagnate; if you remove all standards, endless friction and conflict will erupt.\
   Just like in the human body, we separate **Hard Bone** and **Flexible Joint**:\
   from early childhood, only the “Hard Bone” is strictly standardized (the interface protocol: understanding how one’s own brain works,\
   verifying cause and effect, and keeping one’s word without manipulation), which permanently removes everyday hostility.\
   The actual content of life—the “Flexible Joint”—remains completely free and unscripted.
2. **The Pulsar of 100 Unique Niches ($1 \to 100 \to 1' \to 10,000$):**\
   In a world where everyone is fed, scarcity shifts to the struggle for status and attention.\
   So that well-fed people do not fight over a single “violin,” the system opens **100 unique creative niches** for every 100 people,\
   turning a brawl into a symphony orchestra (Win-Win).\
   And to keep narrow specialists from losing a common language (the “Tower of Babel” effect),\
   a breathing cycle operates: an exhale into diversity ($1 \to 100$) is followed by an inhale ($100 \to 1'$)—\
   compressing the best discoveries into an updated common educational foundation.
3. **The Evolution of Exchange: From “Flowing Energy-Blood” to a Direct Needs Network and Personal Weight:**\
   * *During the growth phase of the new environment:* Instead of banks charging compound interest, a **Flowing Energy Unit** circulates through the network’s vessels (pegged to the baseline energy cost of goods in kWh, plus a voluntary bonus from the buyer for talent and mastery).\
     It carries zero usurious interest and slowly “cools” over time if hoarded as dead treasure or used to stockpile industrial metals in dead warehouses (*demurrage*), keeping resources in motion.\
     If a chain of middlemen becomes overgrown with bureaucratic swamp, any master with a shorter, cleaner path captures the order flow directly (**The Law of Direct Breakthrough**), while automatically contributing a share to **Feed the Roots** (roads, power stations, and fundamental science).\
     Meanwhile, the Earth’s mineral wealth and prime coastal shores belong to all humanity in a flowing regime (only the clean labor of extraction is paid for).
   * *In the mature future of abundance (A World Without Money):* When automation creates a surplus of basic goods, money withers away—baseline necessities are delivered upon direct request through the network.\
     And the right to gather resources for major projects across parallel contours is granted by non-inheritable **Personal Weight of a Master** (the living peer recognition of fellow builders),\
     where 20% of resources are always open to youth sandboxes without asking permission from elders, and if a student surpasses their teacher, the Master receives a lifelong multiplier of honor—**“The Teacher’s Glory.”**
4. **The Urban School-Technopark for 1,000 Children and the Unbroken Family Nest:**\
   We preserve the scale of the city for genetic diversity and talent discovery, but reassemble it from within:\
   50–100 families form a warm **Family Courtyard** around an open district **School for 1,000 children**.\
   In this school, there are no 6 hours of silent rote memorization at desks: for 2 hours in the morning, each child masters theory at their own pace on an adaptive trainer,\
   and for 4 hours in the afternoon, they work in **mixed-age squads of ten** across 100 workshops, where older children teach younger ones (and at ages 16–22, youth travel on inter-city exchanges).\
   Even if parents divorce, a child’s world in such a Courtyard does not shatter by a single millimeter, and raising healthy, wise children is valued and rewarded on par with the highest scientific achievements.

**How do we overcome inertia and launch the system today? (Step 0%, “Rat Park,” the “Legal Spacesuit,” and the Council of Scientists)**\
Why do people today sit on the couch trapped in screen addictions?\
The famous **“Rat Park”** experiment proved it: a rat drinks drugged water to death only in an empty, solitary cage;\
in a spacious park with companions, play, and live interaction, it voluntarily rejects the narcotic!\
We build a **“Zero-Friction Bridge”** that begins with **Step 0% (a single person starting today)**:\
stepping out of cheap dopamine addictions, keeping one’s word 100%, growing in mastery at work, and finding 2–3 reliable allies.\
From there, the neighborhood courtyard (scaling from 1% wholesale farm-food co-ops to a 100% technopark) pulls the urban dweller out of their concrete solitary cell with **three immediate incentives in the very first month**:\
cutting family living costs in half, rescuing them from loneliness in a warm circle of friends, and offering genuine respect from neighbors.

There is no need to fight states: just as the **smartphone** peacefully entered everyone’s life over 15 years simply through superior convenience, our network gently grows right through the old world.\
To keep bureaucracy from striking the first sprouts, a **“Legal Spacesuit”** (100% legal forms of the old world) is used at launch:\
internal exchange operates as a *consumer cooperative and loyalty points* (with external taxes paid automatically through a gateway), the school operates as *homeschooling (external exams) and hobby clubs*, and the Court of Masters operates as *pre-trial mediation*.\
Meanwhile, the network helps poorer nations not through a destabilizing wave of mass migration, but by **exporting “Seeds of Autonomy”**—open technologies for water desalination, solar power, agro-complexes, and schools, empowering people to build prosperity right in their homeland.\
And an open **Council of Scientists** guides technology across three stages:\
from lightweight algorithms running on existing older chips and distributed micro-factories\
to the natural principle of **molecular self-assembly** (just as inside an ordinary bird’s egg at room temperature, without $20 billion factories, a living chick with eyes and a brain assembles itself in 21 days)—protected by strict *hardware immunity in baseline cartridges* that physically prevents lone actors from synthesizing pathogens or explosives.

### 6. What Should a Human Do? (Personal Manifesto)

I am a human being.\
I am a part of the Universe and its growing consciousness.\
My task is to free my mind from animal fear and the illusions of “Plato’s Cave,” keep my word,\
and bring order and support to the world around me without killing its natural flexibility:\
to experiment, ascend to the peaks of mastery, raise children, and create viable structures\
that will become autonomous, self-repairing invariants for a more complex and grander system.

### 7. What Should Humanity Do? (Strategy)

1. **Individual Autonomy:**\
   The first challenge is to eliminate the baseline fear of survival and raise psychologically and materially self-reliant people.
2. **From Cell to Organism (The Individual Before Society):**\
   First, we secure the stability and well-being of each individual “cell” (the person, the Family Courtyard, the Technopark),\
   and only from them, bottom-up, does a healthy “organism” assemble (a planetary network without a central “throne”).
3. **Optimization and Honest Exchange:**\
   Transitioning from usury to flowing energy-blood, and ultimately to a direct Needs Network and the non-inheritable authority of the Master.
4. **Antifragility and Harmony of Standard and Freedom (“Bone and Joint”):**\
   A unified childhood standard of honesty and clear thinking paired with complete freedom of life path (“The Trail, the Forest, and the Wild Frontier”) and preservation of the “Right to Silence.”
5. **Holding Nature’s Attractor and Spatial Immunity:**\
   Protecting the biosphere with a wild-nature buffer framework, and defending society with the dual-mode “Ice / Fog” shield and the export of “seeds of development” across the planet.

### 8. What Should the Universe Do? (Cosmic Super-Combinatorics and Open Source)

Inanimate matter has no goal,\
yet through the emergence of life and intelligence, the Universe acquires a vector—\
it begins to know, order, and complexify itself.\
Just as the 4 simple letters of DNA became the universal axiom for millions of species on Earth,\
humanity, having unfolded the fan of its creative niches, in symbiosis with Superintelligence (where the living human serves as the heart of meaning and source of will) will become the foundational axiom\
for **super-combinatorics on a cosmic scale**:

1. Living **Life** on Earth in safety, mastery, and well-being.
2. Mastering **Planets** (a new “exhale” and the birth of a fan of intelligent species adapted to different worlds).
3. Mastering the energy of **Stars**.
4. Mastering **Black Holes** and the fabric of the Substrate itself.
5. Uniting the scattered matter of the Universe into a single sentient network (an “inhale”).
6. **The Seed of a New Reality:** At the end of the cycle, when the Universe begins to cool into Absolute Zero,\
   packing all accumulated memory, beauty, and laws of creation into a super-dense quantum **Seed** (like the acorn of an ancient oak),\
   to preserve the memory of being across the renewal of the cosmic cycle.
