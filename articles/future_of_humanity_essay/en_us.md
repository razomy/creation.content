# A Vision for the Future of Humanity

## Principles

- Take the best of what works right now, \
  formalize it, and scale it.
- Do not fight the old world; instead, create a system with higher efficiency \
  that integrates other forms through an evolutionary path.

## Key Questions

1. What have we already tried?
2. What do we want, and why did it fail?
3. How do we solve the problem based on the principles of physics?
4. What does a human need in order not to be a beast?
5. How do we structure the new society, and how do we begin?
6. What should the individual do?
7. What should humanity do?
8. What should the Universe do?

### 1. What have we already tried? (Critique of existing systems)

**Every historical system carries within it the gene of its own destruction:**

**Religions and Philosophies (Managing Meaning)**

* **Polytheism (Paganism):** tribal fragmentation due to the lack of a unified law and universal morality.
* **Monotheism:** a monopoly on "absolute truth," inevitably breeding fanaticism, dogmatism, and religious wars.
* **Confucianism:** developmental paralysis and the death of innovation due to the cult of the past, hierarchy, and the dictate of ritual over substance.
* **Taoism and Buddhism:** social escapism and defenselessness in the face of external, well-organized aggression.

**Forms of Government (Managing Power)**

* **Autocracy / Monarchy:** the ruler losing touch with reality (due to being surrounded by flatterers) \
  and bloody crises during the transfer of power.
* **Totalitarianism / Fascism:** self-destruction in an endless search for external and internal enemies, \
  without which the system cannot hold onto power.
* **Theocracy:** stagnation from attempting to govern a changing world by unchangeable laws from a thousand years ago.
* **Democracy:** short-sightedness (thinking from election to election) and degeneration into populism and the manipulation of crowd emotions.
* **Meritocracy:** simulating competence for the sake of a career (gaming KPIs) and turning the successful into a new closed caste.
* **Technocracy:** dehumanization—reducing a living human being to dry numbers, algorithms, and profitability.

**Economics and Ideologies (Managing Resources)**

* **Feudalism (Caste system):** the degeneration of elites without competition \
  and the loss of 90% of talent due to blocked social mobility.
* **Capitalism:** the dead end of infinite growth on a planet with finite resources, \
  leading to monopolies and crises of overproduction.
* **Communism:** suppression of the individual, the death of initiative due to forced egalitarianism, \
  and the inability of a central authority to calculate the needs of all people.
* **Social Democracy (Welfare State):** a crisis of dependency, \
  falling birth rates, and the loss of the will to strive due to an excess of comfort.
* **Libertarianism / Anarchism:** a power vacuum \
  that, instead of a "free market," is instantly filled by the strongest predators (cartels, monopolies, gangs).

**Main Conclusion:** Every system collapses because it elevates one facet of human nature to an absolute\
(faith, profit, equality, order, or freedom) while ignoring the others. \
Over time, the suppressed part of human nature blows the system up from within.

### 2. What do we want, and why did it fail?

We want two things: **security and development**.\
Yet the paradox is that the development of some often leads to the suppression of others through the control of resources and another's will.

**Why did past systems fail?**\
At the core of any complex social system are people.\
At the core of a human is biology.\
And biology obeys the laws of physics and evolution.\
Evolution requires diversity for the survival of a species, which inevitably breeds competition.\
Biology is blind: it creates people with all kinds of deviations— \
both geniuses who drive technology forward and tyrants who oppress the people.

We cannot fully control biology and physics, as we ourselves are their result.\
Entropy can destroy even the most stable system at any moment.

According to the law of normal distribution, **99% of people** will remain within the norm and maintain stability.\
But **1% of radical outliers** (both positive and negative)\
are capable of coming to power and steering the entire system into chaos or into its next form.

By creating rigid rules and prohibitions (as in Confucianism or totalitarianism), \
we automatically create a force of resistance, which makes the system fragile.

### 3. How do we solve the problem based on the principles of physics? (Chaos and Invariants)

We cannot abolish human nature, but we can minimize the risks.\
In physics, we can distinguish two principles: \
**Chaos** (the unpredictable) \
and **Invariant** (a stable structure that preserves itself amidst surrounding changes).\
And they complement each other: the existence of a living invariant requires the constant motion of chaos.

Cities, businesses, the laws of nature—these are invariants.\
Progress, optimization, the right to make mistakes—this is movement through chaos, the process of creating new, more advanced invariants.\
Our task is not to fight chaos, \
but to design flexible structures capable of feeding on it and sustaining themselves over the long run:\
from communication and education to autonomous cities and terraforming.

#### How to create invariants?

1. **Fractality (From cell to organism):** \
   The system must be composed of self-sufficient, autonomous units:\
   *Individual → Community → Settlement → City → Humanity*.\
   Just as healthy cells form a healthy organism.\
   This is the natural course of nature—analogous to how trees grow:\
   starting from a small seed, the plant orders the world around itself into a stable form,\
   fills the space with leaves, covers the surface, and exists under the sun for hundreds of years.
2. **Obviousness (The path of least resistance — Environment Architecture and "The Forest"):** \
   An invariant is maintained not by prohibitions, but by the environment.\
   Imagine a footpath from point A to point B.\
   99% of people will walk in a straight line because it is convenient (obvious, optimal).\
   For the 1% of enthusiasts and rebels, around the path there should be metaphorical "dense forests and cliffs"—\
   a multi-layered system of overcoming complexity.\
   Stepping beyond the bounds is possible, but it will require colossal effort and intellect.
3. **Accessibility (Naturalness of rules):**\
   A reliable invariant rests on physics and empiricism that is understandable to a human (the flow of energy).\
   The cycle of day and night is an ideal invariant.\
   In society, rules must work as inexorably and understandably as gravity.\
   The rule "do not touch fire—you will get burned" works because a person feels pain.\
   The rules of society must have the same direct and obvious feedback, rather than being abstract laws on paper.

**What is "The Forest"?**\
It is a life path of trial and error, like the path of the Tao or playing through a complex video game,\
where you are given situations that teach you about problems, their consequences, and set a vector for development.\
Since the system educates a person from childhood, it can find their pain points and, within the framework of education,\
guide them through a deep understanding of what it means to be human: provide simulations of loyalty and friendship, allow them to experience pain and grief.\
We already go through this haphazardly in life, but by creating such an environment consciously,\
we guarantee that everyone walking toward the summit will receive the full spectrum of human experience and overcome their fears\
(for it is fear that most often governs a person and turns them into a tyrant).\
The longer and harder this path, the higher the chance that a person will reach the goal with awareness\
and treat both their own path and the world as a whole with responsibility.

**How to guard against a closed elitist rule?**\
The first to master "The Forest" will begin to form local power, \
and worse still—they might try to monopolize access to it.\
And this will be the primary task of the architect: to ensure that **at the top of the system there is no "throne" or master switch** that can be seized.\
Therefore, we must introduce a direct, decentralized vertical of access— \
like with a smartphone, where everyone has direct access to everything (1-to-1), but within a multi-level and multi-layered system.\
Filtration must happen through complexity and scale, \
which by its very architecture will strive for diversity.\
Where the growth of power automatically leads to an increase in complexity and responsibility— \
like the stone of Sisyphus: the higher you climb, the more effort you must expend, and if you attempt usurpation, the network simply flows around you like water around a stone.

**Is this manipulation?**\
The modern world, built on capitalism, already uses propaganda and manipulation as its primary engine— \
so this is not the creation of a new evil, but the correction and redirection of what already exists.\
Yes, this is the creation of an artificial environment and obstacles.\
But it is precisely a complex environment that compels a persistent person to develop their intellect and soul in order to step beyond its bounds.\
It is a natural filter that selects the best and channels their energy into creation, \
reducing the risk of destroying the system to a minimum.

**Who is the architect of "The Forest," and isn't this the Matrix?**\
Isn't society already a matrix right now?\
Today, a person is shaped from childhood by advertising,\
propaganda, and outdated institutions, but they do so chaotically and in the interests of others.\
We are not creating coercion from scratch—we are replacing a blind and broken environmental architecture with a conscious and **open one (Open Source)**.\
Moreover, the complexity of this "Forest" does not come out of thin air and does not depend on the whim of a single creator: it grows naturally,\
in direct proportion to the growing complexity of society itself and its technologies.

The architect is everyone who takes on this task and walks this path themselves, building upon the system. And yes, in some sense, it is a Matrix.\
The problem with classical meritocracy is that power there is determined by the subjective assessment of other people.\
Here, the filter is the complexity of the system itself: \
you cannot change the system without possessing an internal complexity equal to the system itself.\
And by comprehending the system on the way to its summit, \
you inevitably educate yourself and change (and the main task of the system is to be educational).\
In fact, I am describing our reality:\
this is the path of any true scientist, warrior, or sage who made themselves, \
arriving at a deep understanding of the human being (power, will, fears, emotions) and nature (physics, biology).

*Example:* Suppose we build the system on the principle of Bitcoin.\
To change the Bitcoin protocol, one must thoroughly understand its entire foundation— \
from cryptography and programming to philosophy and economics. \
Then, one must introduce changes in a test environment (a sandbox) and run them through thousands of checks,\
where each test solves a specific problem. \
And only when the code proves its viability in the sandbox and is voluntarily accepted by other nodes,\
is it scaled across the entire network.

**How to prevent a radical or a tyrant from breaking the entire system? (The Principle of New Taoism)**\
The main danger of a tyrant or psychopath lies not only in their biological deviation,\
but in the narrowness of their experience and understanding of the world. \
A tyrant breaks the system because they think narrowly. \
When the system guides such a person through a deep experience of living through another's pain,\
friendship, and shows them the full beauty and complexity of the world order, \
their predatory energy is transformed. \
Understanding the system as a whole, even an absolute egoist realizes \
that creating and adding complexity to the world is simply more profitable and interesting than destroying it.

We must understand that the modern system is many times more fragile than the proposed one:\
today, a single authoritarian vertical with a nuclear button can destroy the entire planet,\
and a tyrant can gather followers and seize power by force while remaining ignorant.\
We, on the other hand, are describing an extremely complex barrier:\
our task is to equip even a potential tyrant with the deepest knowledge, experience, and understanding.\
Let the tyrant walk this path—after such a transformation, it will be far safer for the world than the dictatorship of a fool.\
But suppose he is a genius of simulation, possesses incredible luck or superintelligence, cheated all the trials, and decided to break or subjugate the system.\
Here, **New Taoism (the principle of Water)** comes into play:
1. **Striking into emptiness:** Because the system is fractal and has no single center ("throne"), the tyrant simply has nothing to seize. In the event of aggression, autonomous cells of society will simply disconnect him from the exchange of energy and resources.
2. **Feeding on chaos:** Any attempt to hack the system serves as a free stress test for it—the system absorbs the blow and automatically becomes more complex at the point of breach, like an immune system.
3. **Evolutionary transition:** If we could not stop him even with this, and he managed to rewrite the base code of the entire network—it means his internal complexity surpassed all of humanity, and the planet was destined to go this way regardless.\
   Perhaps these changes are truly needed by evolution;\
   otherwise, the architecture of the environment simply would not have allowed it to happen.\
   And then, the formation of a new universal environment will begin.\
   At present, there is no way to insure a system 100% (short of total singularity),\
   but 99% protection will cover all known risks of our reality.\
   Meanwhile, the very development of people within the system will stimulate them to maintain and strengthen it.

### 4. What does a human need in order not to be a beast?

1. **Foundation (Biological minimum and natural filtering):** \
   Food, water, air, shelter, safety, and the opportunity to reproduce.\
   These are basic axioms.\
   Society is obligated to guarantee them to everyone by birthright through automation, robotics, and cheap energy,\
   so that this does not become a burden on the labor of other people; and where human labor is still necessary,\
   it must be organized as a self-regulating and self-sustaining system with a cyclical rotation of roles.\
   Without covering basic needs and fears, you cannot get an intellectual human being.\
   A human without intellect, driven by the fear of survival, is a beast, a wolf, driven only by hunger and force.
2. **Intellect:** \
   Education and a vector for development. \
   Everyone must have access to tools for self-realization, achieving goals, and exploring "The Forest."\
   The environment itself should gently guide participants toward development and finding their own path.\
   Like books, through which we empathize and comprehend the world alongside the heroes, \
   or like simulators and schools, where we learn to drive a car step by step.
3. **Spirituality and Ethics:** \
   The best model for understanding the material world is the scientific method. \
   And for things unknowable, transcendent, and existential, we need spirituality,\
   culture, and ethics—as a pillar for the human psyche (the "soul").
4. **Channeling deviations and competition:** \
   We must acknowledge all manifestations of human nature, \
   but confine them within safe (predictable) boundaries (sandboxes). \
   People craving power and dominance must be given complex competitive environments— \
   from business and science to hundreds of types of virtual worlds and games with clear rules. \
   The more such "arenas" exist for realizing ambitions, the lower the risk \
   that these people will go out to destroy the real world for the sake of power. \
   We divert destructive energy into a safe channel.

**What if, having received this comfort, people do not want to develop and begin to degrade?** \
First: why is this degradation, and not another form of development? \
Second: let them degrade (thanks to the automation of basic goods, they do not burden the system)— \
in this case, the system will naturally and without wars contract down to the active core of people\
who will not degrade and for whom simple comfort is too cramped.\
And if no such core is found—then there was never any meaning in the existence of humanity in the first place.

### 5. How do we structure the new society, and how do we begin?

The system of humanity must absorb all new optimizations and remain flexible to change.\
The nature of entropy and chaos is the dissipation of energy, generating a difference in potentials and environments.\
The task of humanity as an intelligent structure is \
to capture and redistribute energy to uniformly maintain life and order.\
Therefore, any optimization of this energy balance is the key to our long-term existence.

Initially, physics and biology made us unequal for the sake of natural selection. \
But humanity has moved to a new stage: **we have created technologies that equalize us physically.** \
Which means that on a basic level, all people can have equal access to resources.

The next frontier is **intellectual equality**. \
Through open knowledge bases (like Wikipedia) and the development of AI, we equalize human cognitive capabilities. \
In such a society, each person takes on a zone of responsibility according to their interests, \
and universal basic equality becomes the foundation for **emergence**—the birth of a new quality of humanity.

**Why won't people need to be forced to develop, and how will we change the old world?**\
By the laws of physics, entropy, and human will: when basic needs and fears are covered,\
the active core of humanity will move forward on its own—progress and the thirst for knowledge cannot be abolished.\
Coercion is unnecessary; the free energy of life naturally seeks application in creation and expansion.\
And to launch this system, there is no need to wage war against states—we just need to start building it.\
If the new system is more efficient than the modern one (and it will be),\
society will reorganize itself and flow into it by the law of conservation of energy, \
and the question of struggling for power will disappear on its own.

Yes, at first the old world may try to ban or block the sprouts of the new system.\
But the history of technology shows: what is banned today out of fear\
is used by everyone tomorrow—simply because it is convenient and gives a colossal advantage.\
Any old structure that rejects high efficiency will simply lose the competition to those who embrace it.

### 6. What should the individual do? (Personal Manifesto)

I am a human. \
I am a part of the Universe and a product of chaos.\
My task is to order and maintain the world around me without killing its natural flexibility:\
to experiment, create new structures, test their resilience, and formalize them. \
To ensure that my creations become autonomous, self-repairing invariants\
that will become part of a more complex and large-scale system.

### 7. What should humanity do? (Strategy)

1. **Individual Autonomy:** \
   The first challenge is to raise psychologically (free from fear) and resource-autonomous people.
2. **The Individual Before Society:** \
   First, we ensure the stability and well-being of each individual "cell" (the human),\
   and only from them is a healthy "organism" (society) formed.
3. **Optimization and Simplicity:** \
   Creating simple and understandable tools for coordination.\
   Everything that can be formalized and automated must be simplified.
4. **Antifragility (Rejecting Rigidity):** \
   Excessive strictness of rules makes a system fragile. The system must be flexible, adaptive, and resilient (like water), \
   and its parts modular: capable of easily disassembling, assembling, and reassembling into new forms.
5. **Chaos Control:** \
   Preventing uncontrolled cascading failures through decentralization.

### 8. What should the Universe do? (Cosmic Expansion)

Inanimate matter has no purpose, \
but through the emergence of life and intelligence, the Universe acquires a vector—\
it begins to comprehend, order, and add complexity to itself. \
We (and the species that come after us) are the self-awareness of the Universe,\
and our path of scaling looks like this:

1. Living **Life** in safety, perfection, and well-being.
2. Mastering **Planets**.
3. Mastering the energy of **Stars** (Dyson spheres or their alternatives).
4. Mastering **Black Holes**.
5. Uniting the scattered matter of the Universe into a single intelligent network.
6. Stepping beyond the bounds of our physics—creating a **New Reality** and comprehending the next mysteries of existence.
